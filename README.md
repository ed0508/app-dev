My Favorite Movie: Avengers: Endgame
Avengers: Endgame is the epic conclusion to the Infinity Saga. It brings together over a decade of storytelling in a massive battle for the fate of the universe.

Why it's a masterpiece:
The Stakes: The heroes have to deal with the aftermath of losing everything.

Time Travel: A clever "Time Heist" that revisits iconic moments from previous films.

The Final Battle: One of the most ambitious action sequences in cinematic history.

Top 3 Moments:
Captain America lifting Mjolnir.

The "Portals" sequence and the Avengers Assemble line.

Iron Man's final sacrifice.

"I love you 3000." — Morgan Stark
